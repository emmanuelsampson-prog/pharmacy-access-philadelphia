# References and source notes

Sources checked on November 16, 2026. See `data/source_manifest.json` for exact retrieval times and data hashes. References describe evidence available; they do not substitute for a verified closure-event ledger.

1. **Pennsylvania Department of State / Department of Health.** [Find Naloxone at a Pharmacy Near Me: Current Statewide](https://data.pa.gov/Opioid-Related/Find-Naloxone-at-a-Pharmacy-Near-Me-Current-Statew/ysdx-4vmi/about_data), dataset `ysdx-4vmi`. The portal reports July 1, 2025 as its last update and labels the dataset public domain. It lists registered pharmacies and notes that some coordinates can be approximate. Registration does not establish active community-pharmacy operation.
2. **U.S. Census Bureau.** [2022 ACS Summary File, table-based format](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/summary-file.2022.html); [official five-year bulk files](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2022/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/). Tables B01001, B01003, B03002, B08201, B17001 and B19013. Public estimates and margins of error, 2018–2022.
3. **U.S. Census Bureau.** [Cartographic boundary files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html), 2022, 1:500,000 Pennsylvania tracts and US counties. Used for geographic joins, centroids and border sensitivity, not navigation.
4. **AmeriHealth Caritas Pennsylvania / Community HealthChoices.** [Rite Aid pharmacy closure notice, June 5, 2025](https://www.amerihealthcaritaschc.com/content/dam/amerihealth-caritas/acpchc/pdf/provider/resources/communications/2025/20250605-rite-aid-pharmacy-closure-notice.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf). Primary insurer communication; references closure or imminent closure and prescription transfers. It is not a store-specific operating-date list. Third-party copyright remains with its issuer.
5. **Philadelphia City Council.** [Resolution 240071](https://phila.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=6503447&GUID=E7438EA1-5E3E-460A-9902-FCDE7D12D48B&FullText=1). Official discussion of 2023 pharmacy closures and access concerns. Context only; not treated as a complete event table.
6. **Penn Libraries.** [Penn Data Access Unlocked via Dewey Platform](https://www.library.upenn.edu/lippincott/datapoints/data-access-dewey-2025), May 21, 2025; [Dewey API access FAQ](https://faq.library.upenn.edu/business/faq/421473), updated July 10, 2026. Student sign-in and data-access guidance; actual product availability is subject to the signed-in catalog.
7. **Advan Research / Dewey Data.** [Foot Traffic / Weekly Patterns dataset record](https://www.deweydata.io/doi/10-82551-x1pp-1f65), DOI 10.82551/X1PP-1F65. The page identifies an older version; resolve and cite the exact version ultimately downloaded. Aggregated visitor patterns are not individual observed routes or mode labels.
8. **Dewey.** [Data access](https://docs.deweydata.io/article/data-access-options) and [Working with Python](https://docs.deweydata.io/article/working-with-python). Instructions for authorized project exports and local analysis. No licensed export has been obtained for this pilot.
9. **Callaway, B., & Sant'Anna, P. H. C. (2021).** Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods. *Journal of Econometrics, 225*(2), 200–230. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001); [authors' methodological guide](https://bcallaway11.github.io/did/articles/multi-period-did.html); [att_gt reference](https://bcallaway11.github.io/did/reference/att_gt.html). The included Python mean-change contrasts are a simpler unadjusted implementation, not their doubly robust estimator.
10. **Butts, K.** [Difference-in-Differences Estimation with Spatial Spillovers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03737). Framework for treatment interference and contaminated comparison groups. Supports explicit exposure mapping and control exclusions; a distance buffer is a substantive assumption, not proof of no spillover.
11. **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.** [Greenhouse Gas Emissions from a Typical Passenger Vehicle](https://www.epa.gov/greenvehicles/greenhouse-gas-emissions-typical-passenger-vehicle). Typical gasoline-vehicle tailpipe factor of approximately 400 g CO2 per vehicle-mile, accessed November 16, 2026. Used only in declared sensitivity scenarios, not as a measured local fleet factor.
12. **SEPTA.** [GTFS developer download](https://www3.septa.org/developer/). Current feed and terms. Historical feeds are needed for a historical transit-access claim; none was used in the pilot.
13. **scikit-learn developers.** [Cross-validation guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html). The implementation uses explicit contiguous coordinate-based folds and a geographic training buffer; preprocessing is fitted inside each fold.
